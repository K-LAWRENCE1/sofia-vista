Clients

Who we work with.

Small teams building their first HR structure, and established businesses that need expertise for a season rather than a salary.

Where we are useful

The work we are usually called in for.

Most engagements start with one of these. If yours looks different, that is worth a conversation rather than a guess.

First structure

A team has grown past the point where things can be handled informally. We write the policies, the contracts and the process — and hand them over in a form you can actually run.

A gap to fill

There is HR work to be done and nobody in-house to do it. We step in for a defined period, do the work, and leave the records in order.

People in transition

Someone is moving into a bigger role, or a team is being restructured. Coaching and change support, so the work carries on through the change.

How it starts

Three steps, no long courtship.

1. A call

You tell us what is not working. We tell you honestly whether it is ours to fix.

2. Scope and fee

We agree what will be delivered, by when, and what it costs, before any work starts.

3. Delivery

We do the work with your team, not around them, and leave the documents behind.

References from previous engagements can be arranged where the client is willing. Ask us.
